## How the narration and the brief name things

These are what the desk calls things, and they apply to every video, every Short and every brief.

**A written essay is an essay.** Never call it a "script". The word "script" must not appear in
any narration, caption or brief text. When the desk refers to a shorter written piece, it is an
"answer", never a "script".

**The person writing it is an aspirant.** Not a student, a candidate or a user, unless the news
itself is about students or candidates.

**The opening stays as it is.** The video opens with the desk's greeting, a word of motivation
and the topics of the day, and why they matter to the aspirant's preparation. Keep that opening
exactly in its present form.
